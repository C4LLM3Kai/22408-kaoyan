01 邀请信Invitation
真题2021 ·2022 ·2025
题干信号
题干出现invite / 邀请⋯⋯参加，并要求交代时间、地点或安排
写作身份
活动组织者、班委或社团负责人；落款写题目指定姓名
必须写到
①活动名称与目的　②时间与地点　③两个环节　④请对方在某日前回复
一句话策略
第一段一句点题，第二段全部交代安排，第三段只做一件事：促回复
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文98 词·7 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①I am writing to invite you to 【活动名称】, which 【主办方】is organizing for 【参加对象】.
②The event will take place 【日期】at 【地点】. ③It will open with 【环节一】, followed by 【环节二】, and
end with 【环节三】. ④There is no charge, and 【附加安排】. ⑤You will also have a chance to 【参与收获】.
⑥I do hope you can join us. ⑦Would you please let me know by 【回复期限】whether you are free that day?
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
段落分工｜①点题｜②—⑤时间、地点、环节、收获｜⑥—⑦促回复
二、真题落地（2022 真题·校园美食节）
Dear Jack,
①I am writing to invite you to our Campus Food Festival, which the Student Union is organizing for international
students.
②The event will take place next Saturday at the Central Square. ③It will open with a short cooking show, followed
by a tasting session, and end with a vote for the most popular dish. ④There is no charge, and light refreshments will
be served. ⑤You will also have a chance to try dishes from over ten provinces.
⑥I do hope you can join us. ⑦Would you please let me know by Wednesday whether you are free that day?
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
三、换装表（只改这几处）
槽位
本篇真题怎么填
可换词块
【活动名称】
our Campus Food Festival
a charity sale / a welcome party
【日期】＋【地点】
next Saturday / the Central Square
next Friday at 7 p.m. / Room 203
【环节一】【环节二】【环
节三】
cooking show →tasting →vote
opening speech →game →free talk
【参与收获】
try dishes from over ten provinces
meet students from other majors
四、阅卷人找什么
• 活动名称、时间、地点三样齐全，且都在第二段，阅卷人一眼能找到
• 两个以上具体环节（不是“interesting activities”这种空话）
• 结尾有一句明确的回复请求，并给出期限
别踩这两个坑
• 只写“下周六”，不写几点、在哪——直接算要点不全
• 通篇I will⋯I will⋯，把邀请信写成日程表
五、想上9 分，从这两句里挑一句加在结尾
• Should you need any further details, please feel free to contact me.
（条件倒装，替代if you need⋯，结尾更像公务信）
• I would be more than happy to reserve a seat for you.
（would be more than happy 比I am happy 礼貌一档）
六、题干变体怎么改（同一篇，换两个词）
• 2021 线上会议：地点改on Zoom，环节改agenda items，附上会议号。
• 2025 短剧：主办方改the Drama Club，参加对象改your classmates。

02 回复邀请·接受Accepting an Invitation
真题2017（回复类真题）
题干信号
题干出现reply to / 回复某封邀请信，第一个动作是接受
写作身份
受邀者；称呼沿用邀请人的身份与姓氏
必须写到
①感谢邀请　②明确接受　③讲座/发言要点　④到场准备或需对方配合的事
一句话策略
第一段必须出现accept 这个词；第二段用“要点＋准备”证明你真的要来
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文98 词·7 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①Thank you for your invitation to 【活动】. ②I am glad to accept it and look forward to 【期待】.
③As you suggested, I will center my talk on 【要点一】and 【要点二】. ④I plan to arrive at 【地点】half an
hour early so that I can 【准备工作】. ⑤If you need any materials from me, please tell me before 【日期】.
⑥Thank you again for thinking of me. ⑦I will do my best to make the talk useful to 【听众】, and I will bring a
short handout.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
段落分工｜①—②感谢＋接受｜③—⑤要点与准备｜⑥—⑦再谢＋承诺
二、真题落地（2017 真题·接受邀请并介绍讲座要点）
Dear Professor Williams,
①Thank you for your invitation to give a presentation on Chinese culture. ②I am glad to accept it and look forward
to meeting your students.
③As you suggested, I will center my talk on traditional festivals and table manners. ④I plan to arrive at the lecture
hall half an hour early so that I can test the projector. ⑤If you need any materials from me, please tell me before
Friday.
⑥Thank you again for thinking of me. ⑦I will do my best to make the talk useful to the international students, and
I will bring a short handout.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
三、换装表（只改这几处）
槽位
本篇真题怎么填
可换词块
【活动】
give a presentation on Chinese culture
attend the seminar / join the panel
【要点一】【要点二】
traditional festivals / table manners
food culture / family life
【准备工作】
test the projector
check the microphone / print handouts
【听众】
the international students
first-year students / the audience
四、阅卷人找什么
• 第一段有明确态度词（accept / delighted to accept），不拖到第二段
• 讲座要点具体到两个名词，不是“something about culture”
• 主动交代到场时间与准备事项，体现“真接受”
别踩这两个坑
• 只写I am glad to accept，后面全是空话，第二个动作没
落地
• 把接受信写成感谢信，通篇thank you，没有安排
五、想上9 分，从这两句里挑一句加在结尾
• I have already prepared a short handout for the audience.
（现在完成时，证明“我真的准备好了”）
• Please let me know if there is anything else I should bring.
（主动询问，显得可靠，不像背模板）
六、题干变体怎么改（同一篇，换两个词）
• 2021 回复邀请：准备工作改test my microphone，日期改the day before。
• 要点部分改成两个具体话题，不要写many interesting things。

03 回复邀请·婉拒Declining an Invitation
真题模拟高频·大纲内
题干信号
题干要求decline / 不能参加，并说明原因或另约
写作身份
受邀者；语气要礼貌，但不能含糊
必须写到
①感谢邀请　②明确不能参加　③一句真实原因　④替代时间或替代方案
一句话策略
先谢、再拒、给原因、给替代——四步缺一不可，尤其不能只拒绝
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文100 词·8 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①Thank you for inviting me to 【活动】. ②I am sorry to say that I shall not be able to attend it, though I really
wish I could.
③The problem is that 【冲突原因】on that very day, and I cannot change the arrangement. ④I feel bad about
missing【期待内容】, which I have been looking forward to. ⑤Could we meet at another time? ⑥I would be free
【备选时间】, and I am happy to 【替代方案】.
⑦I hope you will understand. ⑧Thank you again for your kindness.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
段落分工｜①—②感谢＋拒绝｜③—⑥原因＋替代｜⑦—⑧致歉收尾
二、真题落地（模拟题·与考试冲突，婉拒研讨会）
Dear Professor Wang,
①Thank you for inviting me to the seminar on urban planning. ②I am sorry to say that I shall not be able to attend
it, though I really wish I could.
③The problem is that I have to sit for an important exam on that very day, and I cannot change the arrangement. ④
I feel bad about missing your talk, which I have been looking forward to. ⑤Could we meet at another time? ⑥I
would be free next Friday afternoon, and I am happy to visit your office then.
⑦I hope you will understand. ⑧Thank you again for your kindness.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
三、换装表（只改这几处）
槽位
本篇真题怎么填
可换词块
【活动】
the seminar on urban planning
the conference / the interview
【冲突原因】
I have to sit for an important exam
I will be away on a field trip
【替代方案】
visit your office then
read your paper and send comments
四、阅卷人找什么
• 拒绝得明确（shall not be able to attend），不是“可能不行”
• 原因只有一句，不展开诉苦；替代方案必须具体到时间
• 语气礼貌但不卑微，不用sorry 开头连写三句
别踩这两个坑
• 只拒绝、不给替代方案——任务只完成一半
• 原因写成一段故事（家里、身体、天气全上），挤掉替
代安排
五、想上9 分，从这两句里挑一句加在结尾
• I would be grateful if we could fix another time that suits you.
（礼貌请求，替代生硬的Can we meet again?）
• I hope this will not cause you too much inconvenience.
（先顾对方感受，婉拒信的关键一句）
六、题干变体怎么改（同一篇，换两个词）
• 只用于“被邀请但去不了”；2018 那种“自己取消约定”用07 道歉信。
• 婉拒教授时保留Professor 姓氏，不要用Dear teacher。

04 建议信Advice / Suggestion
真题2011 ·2016 ·2019 ·2023 ·2024
题干信号
题干出现advice / suggestions / which one / 征求意见，要你给主张
写作身份
朋友、同学或下属；同级用商量口气，对师长用I would suggest
必须写到
①明确建议哪一个　②两条理由　③一条附加好处　④把决定权还给对方
一句话策略
第一段一句话给结论，第二段两条理由各一句，第三段客气收尾
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文99 词·8 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①You asked me for my advice on 【咨询事项】. ②I would suggest 【建议对象】.
③For one thing, 【理由一】. ④For another, 【理由二】. ⑤Besides, 【附加好处】, which 【对方获益】.
⑥Of course, the final choice is yours. ⑦If you decide to go, I would be happy to【陪同安排】, and I am sure you
will enjoy it. ⑧I have seen the poster, and it looks really attractive.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
段落分工｜①—②给结论｜③—⑤两条理由＋附加好处｜⑥—⑦交还决定权
二、真题落地（2023 真题·艺术展还是机器人展）
Dear David,
①You asked me for my advice on which of the two events is worth going to. ②I would suggest the robot show.
③For one thing, it displays the latest home robots, which are close to our daily life. ④For another, visitors can try
operating a simple robot themselves. ⑤Besides, the show is held indoors, which means the weather will not matter.
⑥Of course, the final choice is yours. ⑦If you decide to go, I would be happy to go with you, and I am sure you
will enjoy it. ⑧I have seen the poster, and it looks really attractive.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
三、换装表（只改这几处）
槽位
本篇真题怎么填
可换词块
【建议对象】
the robot show
the art exhibition / the online course
【理由一】【理由二】
close to daily life / hands-on
free of charge / good for beginners
【陪同安排】
go with you
help you book the ticket
四、阅卷人找什么
• 第一段就给出明确选择，不写“both are good”这种和稀泥
• 两条理由不重复：一条讲内容，一条讲体验或成本
• 结尾把决定权交还对方，语气不命令
别踩这两个坑
• 只给建议不给理由——2023、2024 都明确要求rea-
son(s)
• 两条理由其实是一条（都说interesting），等于只有一
条
五、想上9 分，从这两句里挑一句加在结尾
• What makes it stand out is the hands-on experience it offers.
（what 从句强调，替代it is very good）
• Either choice would be fine, but this one fits your interest better.
（让步＋比较，语气成熟，不武断）
六、题干变体怎么改（同一篇，换两个词）
• 2019 辩论：建议对象换成a specific topic，理由换成relevance 与data。
• 2024 调查计划：建议对象换成the plan itself，理由讲可行性与时间。

05 感谢信Thanks
真题2010 ·2016
题干信号
题干出现thank / grateful / 感谢对方的接待、帮助或祝贺
写作身份
受助者；对师长正式，对朋友可暖一些
必须写到
①感谢的具体事项　②一处细节回忆　③这件事对你的影响　④回请或祝福
一句话策略
不要只说thank you——用“一个细节”证明感谢是真的
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文103 词·7 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①I have been back in 【地点】for a week, but I still think of the days I spent in 【对方城市】. ②I am writing to
thank you for 【感谢事项】.
③Without your help, 【假设后果】. ④What impressed me most was 【具体细节】. ⑤I have told my family
about it, and they all hope to meet you one day.
⑥By the way, you are welcome to visit【我的城市】at any time. ⑦I would be glad to【接待安排】and introduce
you to my family.
Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
段落分工｜①—②背景＋感谢｜③—⑤影响＋细节｜⑥—⑦回请
二、真题落地（2010 真题·感谢接待并欢迎访华）
Dear Mr. Smith,
①I have been back in Beijing for a week, but I still think of the days I spent in New York. ②I am writing to thank
you for your warm reception during my stay.
③Without your help, I could hardly have settled down so quickly. ④What impressed me most was the dinner you
prepared for me on my first night. ⑤I have told my family about it, and they all hope to meet you one day.
⑥By the way, you are welcome to visit China at any time. ⑦I would be glad to show you around Beijing and
introduce you to my family.
Yours sincerely,
Zhang Wei
三、换装表（只改这几处）
槽位
本篇真题怎么填
可换词块
【感谢事项】
your warm reception during my stay
your help with my application
【具体细节】
the dinner on my first night
the books you lent me
【接待安排】
show you around Beijing
take you to my hometown
四、阅卷人找什么
• 感谢落到具体事上（接待、修改文书、借书），不是空洞的your help
• 有一处细节回忆，这是8 分以上的分水岭
• 结尾有回请或祝福，完成“礼尚往来”
别踩这两个坑
• 全篇thank you so much，没有一件具体的事
• 感谢信用了过去时又突然跳到I will，时态来回跳
五、想上9 分，从这两句里挑一句加在结尾
• Your kindness made a stranger feel at home.
（make sb. feel at home，比warm reception 更有画面）
• I hope I can return the favour when you come to China.
（return the favour 是“还人情”的地道说法）
六、题干变体怎么改（同一篇，换两个词）
• 2016 回复祝贺：开头改Thank you for your congratulations，再接建议。
• 感谢对象是机构时，称呼用Dear Sir or Madam，落款仍用题目姓名。

07 道歉信Apology
真题2018
题干信号
题干出现apologize / 取消、迟到、弄丢、不能赴约，并要求补救
写作身份
失约方；对师长正式，对朋友可少一点公文腔
必须写到
①为何道歉　②一句原因　③补救方案　④另约时间或替代办法
一句话策略
道歉—原因—补救—另约，四句四件事，原因绝不超过一句
一、整篇模板（背这一篇）
正文97 词·9 句
Dear 【称呼】,
①I am writing to apologize for 【道歉事项】. ②I am afraid I have to 【取消/推迟】at such short notice.
③The reason is that 【原因】. ④I know this will cause you trouble, and I am truly sorry. ⑤To make up for it, I
would like to【补救方案】. ⑥Would【备选时间】suit you? ⑦I can also【替代安排】if that is more convenient.
⑧Please accept my apology. ⑨I hope we can meet soon.
