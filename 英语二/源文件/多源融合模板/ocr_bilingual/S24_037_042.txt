•

2.在句首或句末充当状语
状语从句的简化形式
1.条件：主从句主语相同
2.方法：省略主语及引导词，改谓语动词为非谓语动词（doing/ done/ to do）

状语从句：In order that I/we can pass the test, all the efforts are worthwhile.
或To pass the test, 1/we feel all the efforts are worthwhile.

状语从句：Built last year, the house has remained unoccupied/ vacant.
I The house, since it was built last year, has remained unoccupied/ vacant.
