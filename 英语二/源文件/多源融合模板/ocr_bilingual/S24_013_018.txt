要注意改写，不要照抄原文词
不需要用〝祝好〞开头，因为别人给你写信，恭喜你就直接恭喜，谢谢他直接谢谢他。
建议信的二段“拉完了”的样子：
There are several practical measures that can help you effectively achieve your goal. First and foremost, you
should.... What is more, you can... Last but not the least, you'd better.......
建议信的二段不要再写一个“建议如下”的中心句了，除非是表面建议，实际原因的文章。
建议信二段一般写三个建议，每个建议+一个拓展。
①先把事情做起来：准备/基础/第一要务
②再把事情做好：具体行动/方法/场景化操作
③最后把效果做稳：反馈/支持/保障/长期机制
建议一：做起来—基础/前提/第一步
核心不是“I suggest..”，而是先找到：什么东西是实现目标的基础—因此具体应该做什么

2014年考研英语一作文
51. Directions:
Write a letter of about 100 words to the president of your university,suggesting how to improve students' physical
condition.
You should include the details you think necessary.
You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead.
范文展示：
Dear President,
I hope this message finds you well! I am Li Ming, a second-year student at our university. I recently came across
an announcement on the campus bulletin board inviting suggestions on how to improve students' physical
condition. I am therefore writing to offer several practical recommendations.
First, adequate nutrition is critical for students to maintain good health, so I suggest that the university canteen offer
more dishes rich in protein and fresh vegetables. Second, to encourage students to exercise regularly, the
university can extend the opening hours of sports facilities. This can make exercise more convenient after class.
