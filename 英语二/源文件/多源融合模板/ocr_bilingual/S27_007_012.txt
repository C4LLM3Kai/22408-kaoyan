第二章 写作方法
一、第一段（2句话）：写信缘由+寒暄/自我介绍+写信目的
今 写信缘由（非必须）
I'm glad that... /I'm so delighted that.. 我很高兴•
I'm glad to receive your letter/email asking me..我很高兴收到你询问我….的信
It is a great honor to inform you that.../I'm honored to do sth. 很荣幸.
I have heard that. # Pris
Thank you for doing sth.感谢你..
寒暄：（私人信件/熟人）（非必须）
Long time no see.
好久不见
How is everything going?
近来可好？
I have arrived home safe and sound.
我已平安到家。（感谢信/道歉信）
Haven't heard from you for long time. 很久没有收到你的消息了。
I hope this letter finds you well.
我希望这封信抵达时，你一切安好。
自我介绍：
I am an undergraduate in ...university.
我是.大学的本科生
I am an employee/staff member of..company. 我是…公司的职员
I am the customer who had supper in your restaurant yesterday.
我是昨天在你店里吃晚餐的顾客。
心 写信目的：
I am writing to../I am writing this letter/email to.. 我写这封信的目的是⋯
I, as the chairman of the Students’ Union, am planning..作为学生会主席，我打算…
I, on behalf of the Students’Union, am writing to ⋯我代表学生会写这封信目的是⋯
On behalf of the Students’Union, I am writing to ..
I, on behalf of the Students ' Union, am writing (this letter) for the purpose of doing...

二、第二段（4句话）
结构：总起句（1句）＋分层阐述（3句）
总起句
Details are as follows. 相关详细信息如下.
Provided below are my personal qualifications. 以下展示的是我的个人条件。
My decision can be explained by the following factors. 以下原因可以解释我的决定。
The following factors can account for my decision. 以下原因可以解释我的决定。
个 分层阐述：
思路1：指令已给出，按指令写。
思路2：原因1，原因2，原因3
#i 3: What..;Why..; how.
^ 逻辑衔接词：
Firstly,...
Secondly,..
Finally,..
To begin with,.. Besides,..
Moreover/Furthermore/In addition,...
三.第三段（2句话）：表示感谢/歉意＋联系方式+期待回信＋祝福
个 建议信/邀请信/投诉信/推荐信
-—表达感谢/歉意/希望
I would be deeply grateful if you...
We would be pleased if you could accept my...
我（们）很高兴，如果你能采纳我的建议/接受我的邀请/尽快处理投诉
I hope you are interested in this activity. 我希望你能对该活动感兴趣。
一希望建议有用
I hope my suggestion/the information above will be helpful to you.
1I hope you find my advice helpful and useful. 我希望以上建议/信息对你有帮助。

—一联系方式
If you are interested in this activity, please contact us no later than this Friday.
如果你对本次活动感兴趣，请于本周五前联系我们。
If you are interested in this activity, please call 12345 or send your application to
123@qq.com.
若你对本次活动感兴趣，可致电 12345，或将申请材料发送至邮箱 123@qq.com。
Feel free to contact me if you have any further questions.
如有其他问题，欢迎随时联系我。
感谢信/道歉信/祝贺信（不是重点）
Again, I would like to express my heartfelt ... to you! Please accept my ...
我将再次表达我对你的感谢/道歉/祝贺
I feel grateful to you beyond words.
我对您的感激难以言表。
I would like to express my sincere appreciation/apology to you once more at the conclusion
of this letter. 我想在结尾处再次向您表示诚挚感谢/歉意。
I am indeed sorry for my mistake/carelessness, and I would be deeply grateful if I can get
your understanding and forgiveness. ：
对于自己的过失/粗心，我确实深表歉意，若能得到您
的理解和原谅我讲不胜感激。
期待回信
I'm looking forward to your reply. 静候佳音
I'm looking forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
期待你的回复
I am looking forward to your participation/joining. 期待你的加入（邀请信/通知）
祝福语
Enjoy yourself on Sunday./Have fun！ 玩的开心！
Wish you a wonderful college life. 祝你大学生活精彩纷呈！
Wish you all the best. 万事如意！
