状语从句
状语从句——最好用、最简单的从句
最对应中文的从句
顺：时间、地点、原因、目的、结果、条件、方式
转：让步、比较
一篇作文里怎么着也会用三四个
1.好用的时间状语从句
> Ifelt thrilled when I received your message, in which you mentioned you succeeded in..（小作文开头写收到
来信）
As competition intensifies in contemporary society， （随着竞争加剧，来引出现在社会背景）
> When young people put too much energy into entertainments, they gradually become less ambitious and
motivated.（讲一种主题论证假设情况下的合理结果）
2.地点状语从句比你想象中少
> Where there is a will, there is a way. 有志者事竟成
> Wherever we go, we should respect the local customs and adhere to public ethics.（不管我们去哪，我们都
该。。）
• Living in a society where people can not live without social media platforms, we. (sia)
3.原因状语从句是最常见的拓展法
• I suggest that you take a trip with your parents, because you will meet them much less after you go to college.
（给建议＋原因拓展）
College students have become less ambitious because they are surrounded by numerous temptations. （现象＋原
因解释）
> The boy on the left decides to give up because of the long journey.（图中行为的解释）
• You must not miss Yuel Mountain, because from there you can enjoy a panoramic view of the entire city of
Changsha.（用原因讲优点）

4.好用的目的状语
很多图的中心句：
• In order to catch up with the trend of globalization, we ought to be more open-minded.
> Weought to be more open-minded, so that we will be able to catch up with the trend of globalization.（这是目
的状语从句）
> In order to progress faster, you can take some online course.（建议的拓展法：我的建议能达到的好结果）
5.结果状语从句，是主系表的升级。
> Persistence is important.
• Persistence is so important that even when faced with obstacles, those who persevere are more likely to
succeed.（后面接完整的句子）
6.学会i，文思泉涌。
• If you intend to find a high-paying job, you should consider going to bigger cities such as Beijing and
Shanghai.（建议信引出假设的好事）
• If you do not have a local bank account, it will be inconvenient for you to make payments in your daily
life.（建议后，给出不听劝的坏结果）
> Ifwe continue to ignore this problem, our environment is likely to face devastation.（英一大作文二段结尾。+
引出三段解决办法）
7.好用的让步状语从句
> Although the cartoon seems simple, it conveys a clear message： .•（英一大作文二段中心句）
